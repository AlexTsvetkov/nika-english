# Lesson 10 — Mimi's day 🐱
We tell Mimi's whole day, from waking up to sleep — this is present simple!

**STORY BOX**
> I get up. I eat. I play with my ball.
> My friend Max runs. He eats too.
> We play all day. We are happy.
> At night I sleep. Goodnight!

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: "Do you…? / Does she…?" questions (review Lesson 9).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say YOUR day" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Tell a whole day (present simple): I get up, I eat, I play, I sleep.
- Words: get up ⏰ (вставать), eat 🍳 (есть), sleep 😴 (спать), night 🌙 (ночь).

**PATTERN BOX** — my day, in order:
> I get up.   I eat.   I play.   I sleep.
> He gets up.   He eats.   He plays.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — mime the day: stretch, eat, play, then sleep.
2. **Day order** — put action cards in order and say each: "I get up… then I eat…"
3. **Mimi and Max** — tell Mimi's line, then Max's with the -s: "I eat. He eats."
4. **Ask me** — Nika asks mum: "Do you get up? Do you eat?" (review Lesson 9).
5. **Now say YOUR day** — Nika tells her real routine: "I get up. I eat. I play. I sleep!"

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: tell your whole day in four sentences, from morning to night. You finished Mimi's story!

> 🇷🇺 Это и есть **present simple** — рассказ о том, что мы делаем каждый день. Два главных правила курса: без am/is/are с действием ("I eat", не "I am eat") и **-s** только для he/she/it ("he eat**s**").
