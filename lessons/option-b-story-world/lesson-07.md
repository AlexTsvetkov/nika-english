# Lesson 7 — Mimi plays 🐱
Mimi loves to play — now we say what WE do, with action words.

**STORY BOX**
> I like to play.
> I run. I jump. I play with my ball.
> Max, come! We play together.
> We run and we jump. Fun!

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: feelings with am/is/are (review Lesson 6).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Say what you do: **I ___ / We ___** (action words).
- Words: play 🎾 (играть), run 🏃 (бегать), jump 🤸 (прыгать), like 💗 (любить).

**PATTERN BOX** — I / we + action:
> I play.   I run.   I jump.
> We play.   We run.   We jump.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — do each action: run on the spot, jump, play.
2. **Action chain** — parent calls an action; Nika does it and says "I run! I jump!"
3. **We do it** — both do the same action together: "We jump! We run!"
4. **Mimi plays** — tell Mimi's lines: "I run. I jump. I play."
5. **Now say it about YOU** — Nika says: "I run. I jump. I play with my ___."

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: do and say three actions — "I run. I jump. I play."

> 🇷🇺 Важное правило present simple: с действием НЕТ am/is/are. Говорим "I **play**", а НЕ "I am play". Просто I + действие.
