# Lesson 4 — Mimi's house 🐱
Mimi shows us her house and the things inside it.

**STORY BOX**
> This is my house.
> It is a house. It is big.
> This is a ball. This is a box.
> I am happy in my house!

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: "He is ___ / She is ___" with photos (review).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Point at things: **This is a ___ / It is ___**.
- Words: house 🏠 (дом), ball 🏀 (мяч), box 📦 (коробка), big 🐘 (большой).

**PATTERN BOX** — this is / it is:
> This is a house.   This is a ball.
> It is big.   It is a box.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — walk around and touch each thing as Mimi names it.
2. **This is a ___** — Nika picks up real objects: "This is a ball. This is a box."
3. **Big or little?** — find big and little things, say "It is big / It is little".
4. **Mimi's house** — look at the story and say: "It is a house. It is big."
5. **Now say it about YOU** — Nika shows her room: "This is my house. This is a ___."

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: point at three things at home and say "This is a ___. It is big / little."

> 🇷🇺 В русском нет артиклей, а в английском перед вещью нужно маленькое **a** — "a ball", "a box". И вещь — это **it** (не он/она).
