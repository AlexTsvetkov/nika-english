# Lesson 2 — Mimi meets a friend 🐱
Mimi meets a dog called Max — and learns to say "you are".

**STORY BOX**
> Hello! I am Mimi.
> Look — a dog!
> Hello! You are Max.
> You are my friend!

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: say "Hello! I am Nika!" (review Lesson 1).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Talk to a friend: **You are ___**.
- Words: dog 🐕 (собака), friend 👫 (друг), you 👉 (ты).

**PATTERN BOX** — I am / you are:
> I am Mimi.   You are Max.
> You are happy.   You are my friend.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — two puppets, Mimi and Max, meet and shake paws.
2. **Point and say** — point at mum: "You are mum!"; point at a toy: "You are Max!"
3. **Mimi and Max** — Nika is Mimi, parent is Max; swap "I am ___ / You are ___".
4. **Friend hello** — Nika greets a toy: "Hello! You are my friend!"
5. **Now say it about YOU** — point at mum and say: "You are my friend! I am Nika!"

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: point at a family member and say "Hello! You are ___. You are my friend!"

> 🇷🇺 По-русски мы говорим просто «ты Макс», а в английском нужна связка **are** — "You **are** Max". И маленькое "you" значит и «ты», и «вы».
