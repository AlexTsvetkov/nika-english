# Lesson 8 — Mimi's friend plays 🐱
When Max or Lily do something, the action word gets a little -s.

**STORY BOX**
> I play with my ball.
> Max plays too. He runs. He jumps.
> My friend Lily is a cat. She plays.
> She runs. He runs. We all play!

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: "I run / I jump / I play" (review Lesson 7).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- For he / she / it, add **-s**: he runs, she plays.
- Words: Lily (a girl cat) 🐈 (кошка Лили), fast 💨 (быстро), too ➕ (тоже).

**PATTERN BOX** — add the little -s:
> I play.   He plays.   She plays.
> I run.   He runs.   She runs.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — act Mimi ("I play"), then Max ("He plays"), stressing the -s.
2. **Add the s** — parent says "I run"; Nika changes it to "He runs" or "She runs".
3. **Who plays?** — point at a boy toy, then a girl toy: "He plays. She plays."
4. **Mimi and Max** — tell it: "I play. Max plays. He runs fast."
5. **Now say it about YOU and a friend** — Nika: "I play. My friend plays. He/She runs."

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: say one line about you and one about a friend — "I play. He/She plays."

> 🇷🇺 Для he/she/it добавляем **-s**: "he play**s**", "she run**s**". Для I/you/we/they — без -s. Это второе большое правило present simple.
