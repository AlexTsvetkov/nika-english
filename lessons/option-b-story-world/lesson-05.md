# Lesson 5 — Mimi's friends 🐱
Mimi and Max are a team — now we learn "we" and "they".

**STORY BOX**
> Mimi and Max are friends.
> We are happy. We play.
> Look — two cats!
> They are my friends. They are happy.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: "This is a ___ / It is ___" with toys (review).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Talk about a group: **We are ___ / They are ___**.
- Words: we 👫 (мы), they 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 (они), two 2️⃣ (два).

**PATTERN BOX** — we / they:
> We are friends.   We are happy.
> They are cats.   They are my friends.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — Nika and parent stand together for "we", point at toys for "they".
2. **We are** — hug a toy and say: "We are friends! We are happy!"
3. **They are** — point at a group of toys: "They are cats. They are happy."
4. **Mimi and Max** — tell the story team: "They are friends. We are friends too."
5. **Now say it about YOU** — Nika about her and a friend: "We are friends. We are happy!"

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: stand with mum and say "We are happy!"; point at toys and say "They are my friends."

> 🇷🇺 «Мы/они» = we/they. И снова не забываем связку **are** — "We **are** friends", "They **are** happy".
