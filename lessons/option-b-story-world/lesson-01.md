# Lesson 1 — Meet Mimi 🐱
A little cat called Mimi says hello and tells us her name.

**STORY BOX**
> Hello! I am Mimi.
> I am a cat.
> I am happy!

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: wave and say "Hello!" to three toys.
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Say hello and tell your name: **I am ___**.
- Words: cat 🐱 (кошка), happy 😊 (счастливый), hello 👋 (привет).

**PATTERN BOX** — swap the last word:
> I am Mimi.   I am Nika.   I am happy.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — parent is Mimi: wave and read each line; Nika repeats and waves too.
2. **Puppet hello** — pick a toy, make it say "Hello! I am ___" with its own name.
3. **Echo game** — parent says "I am happy", Nika copies with a big smile.
4. **Name chain** — go round the room: each toy says "I am ___".
5. **Now say it about YOU** — Nika waves and says: "Hello! I am Nika. I am happy!"

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: wave and say "Hello! I am Nika!" to mum, dad, and a toy.

> 🇷🇺 В русском нет глагола-связки в настоящем времени: мы говорим «Я Ника», а в английском всегда нужно **am** — "I **am** Nika". Это главное новое слово урока.
