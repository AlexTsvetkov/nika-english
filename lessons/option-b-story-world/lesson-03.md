# Lesson 3 — Mimi's family 🐱
Mimi shows us her family — and we learn "he" and "she".

**STORY BOX**
> This is my family.
> She is my mum.
> He is Max.
> He is my friend. She is happy.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: "I am ___ / You are ___" with a toy (review).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Talk about people: **He is ___ / She is ___**.
- Words: family 👨‍👩‍👧 (семья), mum 👩 (мама), dad 👨 (папа).

**PATTERN BOX** — he / she:
> He is Max.   He is happy.
> She is Mimi.   She is my mum.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — point at a "boy" toy for "he" and a "girl" toy for "she".
2. **He or she?** — parent points at people and toys; Nika says "he" or "she".
3. **Photo talk** — Nika points at a real family photo: "She is my mum. He is my dad."
4. **Mimi's family** — tell two lines about Mimi: "She is Mimi. He is Max."
5. **Now say it about YOU** — Nika says: "She is my mum. He is my dad. I am Nika."

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: show a family photo and say "She is my mum. He is my dad."

> 🇷🇺 «Он/она» легко: he/she. Но английский добавляет **is** — "She **is** mum". И запомни: артиклей, как в русском, в английском нет, зато есть am/is/are.
