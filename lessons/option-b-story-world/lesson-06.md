# Lesson 6 — Mimi feels… 🐱
Max loses his ball and feels sad — we learn feelings with am, is, are.

**STORY BOX**
> I am happy today.
> Max is sad. Why?
> His ball is lost. He is sad.
> Now we are happy — the ball is here!

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: "We are ___ / They are ___" (review).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Say how someone feels: **am / is / are** + a feeling.
- Words: sad 😢 (грустный), hungry 🍽️ (голодный), tired 😴 (усталый).

**PATTERN BOX** — one little word changes:
> I am happy.   He is sad.   We are hungry.
> She is tired.   They are happy.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — make a happy face, then a sad face, as you read.
2. **Face cards** — show a feeling face; Nika says "He is sad / She is happy".
3. **Pick the word** — parent says I / he / we; Nika adds the right word: am / is / are.
4. **Mimi and Max feel** — tell the story feelings: "Mimi is happy. Max is sad."
5. **Now say it about YOU** — Nika says how she feels now: "I am happy. I am hungry!"

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: say how three people feel — "I am ___. Mum is ___. We are ___."

> 🇷🇺 Это одно маленькое слово, которое меняется: **I → am, he/she/it → is, you/we/they → are**. Выучи как песенку — это сердце английского.
