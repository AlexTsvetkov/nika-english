# Lesson 9 — Does Mimi…? 🐱
Max asks lots of questions — we learn to ask with "do" and "does".

**STORY BOX**
> Max asks: Do you like fish?
> Yes, I do! I like fish.
> Does Mimi play? Yes, she does.
> Does Max run? Yes, he runs fast!

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. 5 min — Warm up: "He runs / She plays" with the -s (review Lesson 8).
2. 7 min — Read and act Mimi's story (Exercise 1).
3. 8 min — Speaking games (Exercises 2–4).
4. 5 min — "Now say it about YOU" (Exercise 5) + homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Ask a question: **Do you ___? / Does she ___?**
- Words: fish 🐟 (рыба), yes ✅ (да), no ❌ (нет).

**PATTERN BOX** — do / does for questions:
> Do you like fish?   Yes, I do.
> Does she play?   Yes, she does.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Read and act Mimi's story** — Max asks, Mimi answers; then swap roles.
2. **Yes or no?** — parent asks "Do you like ___?"; Nika answers "Yes, I do / No".
3. **Ask about Mimi** — Nika asks: "Does Mimi play? Does Max run?" and answers.
4. **Ask me** — Nika asks mum: "Do you like fish? Do you run?"
5. **Now say it about YOU** — Nika answers real questions: "Do you like ___? Yes, I do!"

## 🏠 Homework
Every day, 5 min: ask a family member two questions — "Do you like ___? Do you play ___?"

> 🇷🇺 Для вопроса английский берёт помощника **do/does** — "**Do** you like…?", "**Does** she…?". В русском мы просто меняем голос, а здесь нужно это слово.
