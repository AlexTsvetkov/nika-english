# Lesson 8 — Review + first story 📖🎉

> No new letter-sounds. Today: read a whole little **story**. Two new whole-word
> "sight words": **the** (say "thuh") and **is** 🟰 (the s says `/z/`). Learn them by sight.
> 🇷🇺 **the** и **is** — слова-«запоминашки», читаем целиком.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — sound speed round, all 13 cards (3 min)
2. Learn sight words **the / is** (4 min)
3. Read the story + exercises (14 min)
4. Write + homework (4 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Read connected text (a story), not just single words.
- Recognise **the** and **is** 🟰 instantly.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Sight word flash.** Flash **the** and **is** 🟰 cards until she says them with no
sounding-out. 10 fast flashes. ⚡

**2. Word relay.** Read & stack:
`cat 🐱 · hen 🐔 · rat 🐀 · man 👨 · dad 👨‍👧 · red 🔴 · hat 🎩 · map 🗺️ · pin 📌 · net 🥅 · hand ✋ · sand 🏖️` 🏆

**3. Read the story** (point under each word; read it twice):

> ### 🐱 The Red Cat
> Dad 👨 is in 📥.
> A cat 🐱 sat 🪑.
> The cat 🐱 is red 🔴.
> The red 🔴 cat 🐱 ran 🏃!
> I 🙋 pat 🤚 the cat 🐱.
> The cat 🐱 sat 🪑 on dad 👨! 😹

**4. Story questions.** "What colour is the cat? Where did it sit?" (English or Russian —
understanding matters, not just decoding.) 🗣️

**5. Draw & label.** Nika draws the red cat on dad; labels it **cat 🐱** and **dad 👨**. 🎨

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Re-read **The Red Cat** aloud once a day (it gets smoother each time!).
- Copy the line **The cat 🐱 is red 🔴.**
