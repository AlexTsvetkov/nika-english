# Lesson 7 — Sounds `m` and `d` 🍫🥁

> Sounds: `m` = `/mmm/`, `d` = `/d/` (short, no «ы» after it).
> 🇷🇺 Хорошо: `m` = «м», `d` = «д» — почти как в русском. Лёгкий урок.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read 4 words (3 min)
2. New sounds `/m/ /d/` (3 min)
3. Reading exercises (15 min)
4. Write + homework (4 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- **m** says `/m/`, **d** says `/d/`.
- Read longer words (hand, sand) and 3-word sentences.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Sound speed round.** Add m, d. All sounds, fast. 🏁

**2. Read the ladder.**
`mat · map · man · mad · dad · did · dim · den · red · rid`
Count them. 🏆

**3. Longer words.** Blend slowly: **hand ✋ · sand 🏖️ · mend · and**. 4 words.

**4. Word families.** **man–mat–map–mad** (change last sound); then **dad–did–dim**.
She reads each. 🔗

**5. Build & read sentences.** Mixed cards:
- **Dad has a map.** 🗺️ · **A man ran.** 🏃 · **I pat a cat.** 🐱

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Read 5 ladder words daily.
- Write from dictation: **mad · dad · hand**.
