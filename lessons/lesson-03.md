# Lesson 3 — Sounds `i` and `n` 🐭✈️

> Sounds: `i` = short `/ɪ/` (as in **sit**, quick — not «и»/«ай»), `n` = `/nnn/`.
> 🇷🇺 Английская `n` = звук «н» (хотя похожа на русскую «п»!).

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read **sat/tap/pat** (3 min)
2. New sounds `/ɪ/ /n/` (4 min)
3. Reading exercises (14 min)
4. Write + homework (4 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- **i** says `/ɪ/`, **n** says `/nnn/`.
- Read many new words with the letters so far: **s a t p i n**.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Sound speed round.** Flash s a t p i n — say each sound. 2 rounds. 🏁

**2. Read the ladder.** Blend each, climb down:
`in · it · sit · sip · tip · tin · pin · pit · nap · pan · tan · ant`
Tick each word she reads. Count them at the end. 🏆

**3. Missing letter.** Write **p_n**, **s_t**, **t_n** — Nika adds the missing vowel (i or
a) and reads the word. 4 cards.

**4. Swap-a-sound chain.** **pin → tin → tip → sip → sit → sat**. Change one sound each
step; she reads the new word. 🔗

**5. Build & read sentence.** Give mixed cards for **I sit.** — she orders and reads, then
does the action (sits). Also try **I nap.** 😴

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Read 5 words daily from the ladder (pick different ones).
- Do 2 "missing letter" cards you make at home.
