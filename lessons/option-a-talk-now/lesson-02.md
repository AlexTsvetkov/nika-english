# Lesson 2 — "You are ___" + "How are you?"
Today's conversation: Nika greets someone and asks how they are.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (4 min):** Say "Hello! I am Nika." from Lesson 1.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach "You are ___" and "How are you?" with the box.
3. **Talk & act (11 min):** Parent asks, Nika answers, then she asks (Exercises 1–4).
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika asks the parent "How are you?"; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- You are ___ (ты ___) to talk ABOUT the other person.
- Question: How 🤔 are you? → answer: I am good 👍 (хорошо) / happy 😊 (счастливая).

## Pattern box — swap the last word
> You are Nika.
> You are big.
> You are happy.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Say it (speaking):** Point at Mama and say "You are Mama." Point at a toy: "You are Teddy."
2. **Swap game (speaking):** "You are ___ (happy / big / good)." Say 3 versions.
3. **Ask back (speaking):** Parent: "How are you?" Nika: "I am good! How are you?"
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Nika: Hello! How are you?
   - Parent: I am happy! And you?
   - Nika: I am good!
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Right now, how are you? Say: "I am ___ (happy / good / big)."

> 🇷🇺 "I am" — про себя, "You are" — про другого. Снова не теряем маленькое слово (am/are). "How" звучит с мягким /h/ — выдох, как «хь», не русское «х».

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: ask 3 people "How are you?" and answer "I am good!"
