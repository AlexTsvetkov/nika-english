# Lesson 7 — "I like ___" / "I can ___"
Today's conversation: Nika tells what she likes and what she can do.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (4 min):** Flashback — "I have a ___" from Lesson 6.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach "I like ___" and "I can ___" (+ verb) with the box.
3. **Talk & act (11 min):** Do the actions (TPR!) as you say them (Exercises 1–4).
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika says what she likes; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- I like ___ (я люблю ___) and I can ___ (я умею ___).
- Verbs: run 🏃 (бегать), jump 🤸 (прыгать), swim 🏊 (плавать), read 📖 (читать).

## Pattern box — swap the verb
> I like cats.
> I can run.
> I can jump.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Say + do it (speaking + TPR):** Say "I can jump!" and jump! Then run, then swim.
2. **Swap game (speaking):** "I can ___ (run / jump / swim / read)." Say 3 with the action.
3. **Likes (speaking):** Say 3: "I like ___ (cats / dogs / books)."
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Parent: Can you jump?
   - Nika: Yes! I can jump! Can you swim?
   - Parent: Yes! I can swim!
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Tell me 3 true things: "I like ___. I can ___."

> 🇷🇺 После "can" и "like" идёт глагол в простой форме, без изменений: I can **run**. Не добавляем окончаний. "run" со звуком /r/ — язык не дрожит, как в русском «р», а мягко загнут назад.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: say 3 sentences and DO the action — "I can ___!"
