# Lesson 5 — We / They
Today's conversation: Nika talks about herself + a friend, and about a group.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (4 min):** Review "It is a ___" from Lesson 4.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach "We are ___" and "They are ___" with the box.
3. **Talk & act (11 min):** Stand together for "we", point at a group for "they" (Exercises 1–4).
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika says 2 sentences; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- We are ___ (мы ___) = me + you together.
- They are ___ (они ___) = other people.
- Words: friends 👭 (друзья), here 📍 (здесь).

## Pattern box — swap the last word
> We are happy.
> We are friends.
> They are here.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Say it (speaking):** Hug Mama and say "We are friends! We are happy!"
2. **Swap game (speaking):** Say 3: "We are ___ / They are ___ (happy / here / big)."
3. **Point game (speaking):** Point at toys in a group: "They are here. They are happy."
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Parent: Where are they?
   - Nika: They are here! We are friends!
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Say about you and a friend: "We are friends. We are happy."

> 🇷🇺 Теперь вся семья «быть»: I **am**, you/we/they **are**, he/she/it **is**. Это одно и то же «быть», но форма меняется — повторяем на пальцах. "They" с мягким th.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: say 3 sentences — one with "We are ___" and two with "They are ___".
