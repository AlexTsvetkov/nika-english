# Lesson 1 — Hello! "I am ___"
Today's conversation: Nika says hello and tells someone her name.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (5 min):** Wave and say "Hello!" to each other and to a toy.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach "I am ___" with the pattern box below.
3. **Talk & act (10 min):** Do Exercises 1–4 with lots of back-and-forth.
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika says 2 sentences about herself; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Greetings: Hello! 👋 (привет) / Hi! 👋 and Bye! 👋 (пока)
- The phrase I am ___ (я ___) to say your name and age.
- Words: happy 😊 (счастливая), eight 8️⃣ (восемь).

## Pattern box — swap the last word
> I am Nika.
> I am happy.
> I am eight.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Say it (speaking):** Wave and say "Hello! I am Nika." Do it 3 times, louder each time.
2. **Swap game (speaking):** Fill the box with YOUR words: "I am ___ (happy / big / eight)."
3. **Toy talk (speaking):** Hold a toy. The toy says "Hello! I am Teddy." Nika answers: "Hello! I am Nika."
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Parent: Hello! I am Mama.
   - Nika: Hello! I am Nika!
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Tell me 2 true things: "I am Nika. I am eight."

> 🇷🇺 В русском нет глагола «быть» в настоящем: мы говорим «Я Ника», а в английском обязательно "am" — I **am** Nika. Не пропускаем "am"!

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: wave and introduce yourself to 3 toys or family members — "Hello! I am Nika. I am eight."
