# Lesson 10 — PRESENT SIMPLE: "My day"
Today's conversation: Nika tells the whole story of her day — and her friend's day.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (4 min):** Flashback — "Do you ___? / I don't ___" from Lesson 9.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach the "My day" chain with the box, acting each verb.
3. **Talk & act (11 min):** Tell your day in order (TPR!) (Exercises 1–4).
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika tells 3 steps of her real day; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Present simple = things I do every day.
- Verbs: get up ⏰ (вставать), eat 🍳 (есть), go 🚶 (идти), sleep 😴 (спать).
- Word: school 🏫 (школа). Remember: she / he adds **-s** (she plays).

## Pattern box — my day, in order
> I get up.
> I eat.
> I go to school.
> I play.
> I sleep.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Say + do it (speaking + TPR):** Act out your day — stretch for "I get up", eat for "I eat", etc. Say all 5 in order.
2. **Swap game (speaking):** Change to your friend: "She gets up. She eats. She plays." (add -s!)
3. **Question time (speaking):** Parent asks "Do you go to school?" Nika: "Yes, I go to school!"
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Parent: What do you do in the morning?
   - Nika: I get up, I eat, and I go to school!
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Tell me your REAL day in 4 sentences: "I get up … I sleep."

> 🇷🇺 Это главная цель курса — рассказ о дне! Напоминаем: про себя — без -s (I eat), про он/она — с -s (she eats). Хвалим Нику: она уже говорит целыми предложениями!

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: tell your whole day in 4–5 sentences, then say your friend's day with -s ("She gets up…").
