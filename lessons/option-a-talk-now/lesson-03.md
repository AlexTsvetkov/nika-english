# Lesson 3 — He / She: introduce people
Today's conversation: Nika introduces her family and friends to someone.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (4 min):** Review "How are you?" from Lesson 2.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach "He is ___" and "She is ___" with the box.
3. **Talk & act (11 min):** Point at photos/toys and introduce them (Exercises 1–4).
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika introduces 2 real people; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- He is ___ (он ___) for a boy; She is ___ (она ___) for a girl.
- Words: boy 👦 (мальчик), girl 👧 (девочка), Mama 👩 (мама), Papa 👨 (папа).

## Pattern box — swap he/she and the last word
> He is Papa.
> She is Mama.
> He is happy.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Say it (speaking):** Point at a boy toy: "He is Teddy." Point at a girl toy: "She is Dolly."
2. **Swap game (speaking):** Say 3: "He is ___ / She is ___ (big / happy / good)."
3. **Photo talk (speaking):** Show family photos. Nika says "She is Mama. He is Papa."
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Parent (pointing at a toy): Who is he?
   - Nika: He is Teddy! She is Dolly.
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Tell me about your family: "She is Mama. He is Papa."

> 🇷🇺 "He" = он (мальчик), "She" = она (девочка) — дети часто путают, тренируем на картинках. "He" с мягким выдохом /h/, не «хи». Для он/она используем **is**, не am/are.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: point at 3 people or toys and say "He is ___" or "She is ___".
