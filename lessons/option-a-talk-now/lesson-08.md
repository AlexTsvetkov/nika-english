# Lesson 8 — He likes / She plays (-s)
Today's conversation: Nika tells what her friend or family member does.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (4 min):** Flashback — "I like ___ / I can ___" from Lesson 7.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach "He likes / She plays" — add -s for he/she.
3. **Talk & act (11 min):** Point at people and say what THEY do (Exercises 1–4).
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika says 2 sentences about a friend; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- With he / she the verb gets a little **-s**: likes, plays, runs.
- Verbs: play ⚽ (играть), like ❤️ (любить), run 🏃 (бегать), read 📖 (читать).

## Pattern box — add -s for he/she
> I play.  →  She plays.
> I like cats.  →  He likes cats.
> I run.  →  He runs.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Say it (speaking):** Point at Mama: "She plays." Point at Papa: "He runs."
2. **Add the -s (speaking):** Parent says "I play" → Nika changes it: "She plays!" Try with run, read.
3. **Swap game (speaking):** Say 3: "He likes ___ / She plays / He reads."
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Parent: What does she like?
   - Nika: She likes cats! He likes dogs!
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Say about you AND a friend: "I play. She plays too."

> 🇷🇺 Главное правило: для он/она к глаголу добавляем **-s**: he play**s**, she like**s**. Это только для he/she/it. Для «я/ты/мы/они» — без -s.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: say 3 sentences about family — "He ___s / She ___s."
