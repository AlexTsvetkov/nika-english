# Lesson 6 — Review am/is/are + "I have ___"
Today's conversation: Nika shows what she has and tells about it.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (5 min):** Flashback — say I am, You are, He is, We are.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach "I have ___" with the box.
3. **Talk & act (10 min):** Show toys and say what you have (Exercises 1–4).
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika says what she has; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Quick review: am / is / are (быть).
- New: I have ___ (у меня есть ___).
- Words: doll 🪆 (кукла), ball ⚽ (мяч).

## Pattern box — swap the last word
> I have a cat.
> I have a ball.
> I have a doll.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Say it (speaking):** Hold a toy and say "I have a cat!" Do it with 3 toys.
2. **Mix review (speaking):** Say one of each: "I am happy. She is Mama. We are friends. I have a ball."
3. **Swap game (speaking):** "I have a ___ (dog / book / doll)." Say 3.
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Parent: What do you have?
   - Nika: I have a cat! And I am happy!
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Tell me 3 real things you have: "I have a ___."

> 🇷🇺 "have" = «иметь / у меня есть». В русском говорим «у меня есть», а в английском — «я имею»: I **have** a cat. Звук /h/ мягкий, не «хэв» с жёстким «х».

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: say 3 sentences about things you have — "I have a ___."
