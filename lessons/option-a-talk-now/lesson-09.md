# Lesson 9 — "Do you ___?" / "I don't ___"
Today's conversation: Nika asks a question and says what she does NOT do.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. **Warm-up (4 min):** Flashback — "She plays / He likes" from Lesson 8.
2. **New words (7 min):** Teach "Do you ___?" and "I don't ___" with the box.
3. **Talk & act (11 min):** Ask-and-answer back and forth (Exercises 1–4).
4. **Wrap-up (3 min):** Nika asks the parent a question; give homework.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Question: Do you ___? (ты ___?)
- Negative: I don't ___ (я не ___).
- Words: yes ✅ (да), no ❌ (нет).

## Pattern box — swap the verb
> Do you like cats?  →  Yes, I like cats.
> Do you swim?  →  No, I don't swim.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
1. **Ask it (speaking):** Ask Mama 3 questions: "Do you like dogs? Do you run? Do you swim?"
2. **Say no (speaking):** Say 3: "I don't ___ (swim / run / read that book)."
3. **Swap game (speaking):** Mix: "Do you ___?" then answer "Yes, I ___" or "No, I don't ___."
4. **Dialogue — act it out (speaking):**
   - Nika: Do you like cats?
   - Parent: Yes! Do you play ball?
   - Nika: No, I don't play ball. I like books!
5. **Say it about YOU (speaking):** Say 1 "yes" and 1 "no" about you: "I like ___. I don't ___."

> 🇷🇺 Для вопроса ставим маленькое "Do" впереди: **Do** you like…? Для «не» — **don't** (= do not): I **don't** swim. В русском одно «не», в английском — целое слово-помощник "don't".

## 🏠 Homework
Every day for 5 min: ask 3 people "Do you like ___?" and say 1 "I don't ___."
