# Lesson 6 — Sounds `h` and `r` 🎩🐕

> Sounds: `h` = soft breath `/h/` (fog a mirror — NOT harsh «х»); `r` = `/r/` (do **not**
> roll it like Russian «р», tongue stays still).
> 🇷🇺 Оба звука трудные для русских — показывайте сами, Ника повторит.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read 4 words (3 min)
2. New sounds `/h/ /r/` (4 min)
3. Reading exercises (14 min)
4. Write + homework (4 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- **h** says `/h/`, **r** says `/r/`.
- Read and build short sentences.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Sound speed round.** Add h, r. Say all sounds. 🏁

**2. Read the ladder.**
`hat · hen · has · hit · hip · rat · ran · rip · hens · rats`
Count them. 🏆

**3. Rhyme families.** Read the chain, Nika hops on each: **hat–rat–cat–pat**. Then
**hen–ten–pen**. "The ends match — that's rhyme!" 🎵

**4. Picture match.** Lay 4 words (hat, rat, hen, cat) + 4 pictures; she reads each word
and matches it. 🖼️

**5. Read & act sentences.**
- **I ran.** 🏃 · **A rat ran.** 🐀 · **Pat the hen.** 🐔 · **A cat sat.** 🐱

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Read 1 rhyme family aloud daily.
- Read 2 sentences to someone and act them out.
