# Lesson 10 — Sounds `u` and `l` ☔🍭  (milestone! 🎯)

> Sounds: `u` = short `/ʌ/` (as in **sun, cup**); `l` = `/lll/` (tongue up).
> 🇷🇺 `u` = короткий `/ʌ/` «а/э»; `l` = «л», мягче русского.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read 4 words (3 min)
2. New sounds `/ʌ/ /l/` (3 min)
3. Reading exercises (15 min)
4. Milestone celebration + homework (4 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- **u** says `/ʌ/`, **l** says `/lll/`.
- Milestone: **17 sounds** known → read sentences & a story independently.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Sound speed round.** Add u, l — all 17 cards. 🏁

**2. Read the ladder.**
`sun ☀️ · run 🏃 · cup 🥤 · cut ✂️ · nut 🥜 · hut 🛖 · mud 🟤 · gum 🍬 · leg 🦵 · let 🆗 · lip 👄 · lot 🔢 · log 🪵 · lap 🦵`
Count them. 🏆

**3. Longer words.** Blend: **lamp 💡 · land 🏞️ · plug 🔌 · slug 🐌 · slip ⚠️**. 5 words.

**4. Read & act sentences.**
- **The sun ☀️ is hot 🔥.** · **I 🙋 run 🏃 up ⬆️.** · **A slug 🐌 is on the log 🪵.**

**5. Mixed reading race.** From all lessons, read 15 words in a row (pick a spread). Time
it; this is her "reading level" check. 🏁🏆

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Read 5 ladder words daily.
- Re-read **The Red Cat** once more — notice how fast she is now!
