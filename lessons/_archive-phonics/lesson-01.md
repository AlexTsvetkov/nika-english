# Lesson 1 — Sounds `s` and `a` 🐍🐜

> Nika knows these letters' *names*. Today she learns the **sounds** they make — the key
> to reading. Say `/sss/` (hiss), not "ess". Say `/æ/` (short, bright, as in **cat**),
> not Russian «а».

## 📋 Plan (≈20 min)
1. Warm-up — hello + the two sounds (3 min)
2. Learn the sounds `/s/` `/æ/` (4 min)
3. Exercises (10 min)
4. Write + homework (3 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- The letter **s** says `/sss/`.
- The letter **a** says `/æ/` (short, open — the **cat** sound).
- First try at **blending** two sounds together.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Say the sound (not the name).** Point to **s** → "/sss/". Point to **a** → "/æ/".
Do it 5 times, faster each round. 🏁

**2. Sound hunt.** Find 3 things that start with `/s/` — sun ☀️, sock 🧦, soup 🍲 — and 3
with `/æ/` — apple 🍎, ant 🐜, hand ✋. Say the sound as you touch each. 🔎

**3. Which sound?** You say a word — sun ☀️, ant 🐜, snake 🐍, apple 🍎 — Nika shouts
`/s/` or `/æ/` for the first sound. 6 words.

**4. First blend.** Write **a s**. Slide your finger slowly: "/æ/…/sss/… **as**!" Then
**s a** → "/sss/…/æ/… **sa**". Not real words yet — this is the sliding move that *is*
reading. Cheer! 🎉

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Say `/s/` and `/æ/` when you see the letters (make 2 cards).
- Find 1 new thing each day for each sound.
