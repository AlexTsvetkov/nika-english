# Lesson 3 — He and She 👩‍👧

> Now Nika talks ABOUT other people — family and friends. She learns he and she
> and the pattern "He/She is ___".

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review "you are" (4 min)
2. New words — he, she, mum, dad, friend (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "He is ___" / "She is ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Pronouns he 👦 (он) and she 👧 (она); grammar "He/She is ___".
- Family and friends: mum 👩 (мама), dad 👨 (папа), friend 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 (друг).
- Describing word: funny 😂 (смешной).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: hat, hen, red. Three words.

**2. Who is it?** Point at photos or around the room: say "He is ___" for boys and men, "She is ___" for girls and women.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> He is my dad.   She is my mum.   He is funny.   She is kind.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "She is ___", then "He is ___", and swap: kind, funny, tall, big.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me about two real people: "She is my mum." / "He is my friend."

> 🇷🇺 English splits «он/она» into he (boys) and she (girls) — choose carefully.
> And the verb is appears again: She is kind, never "She kind".

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each day say one "He is ___" and one "She is ___" about real people.
- Point at family photos and name each: "He is ___. She is ___."
