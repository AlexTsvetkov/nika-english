# Lesson 17 — Food and drink 🍎

> Nika talks about food: "I want ___" and "Do you like ___?"

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review -ing (4 min)
2. New words — food and drink (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "I want ___" / "Do you like ___?" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Ask for food: "I want ___".
- Ask and answer "Do you like ___?"
- Words: milk 🥛 (молоко), bread 🍞 (хлеб), water 💧 (вода), banana 🍌 (банан).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: milk, bread, water. Three food words.

**2. Snack time.** Point at real or pretend food and say: "I want milk. I want bread."

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I want water.   I want a banana.   Do you like milk? Yes, I do.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "I want ___" and swap in: milk, bread, water, a banana. Four sentences. Then ask me "Do you like ___?"

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me three real foods you like and one you don't: "I like ___ . I don't like ___ ."

> 🇷🇺 "Do you like...?" and "I don't like..." use do/don't from Lesson 9 — same
> helper, new words. English has no articles rule for drinks: "I want water"
> (no "a"), but "I want a banana" (a countable one). Keep it light — she will
> catch the feel with practice.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- At meals say: "I want ___ ."
- Ask a parent: "Do you like ___?" and answer back.
