# Lesson 6 — Am, Is, Are + I have 🧩

> A big review! Nika pulls together am / is / are, then learns a brand-new verb:
> have — "I have ___".

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + quick am/is/are review (4 min)
2. Review all pronouns + to be (5 min)
3. New word have — pattern "I have ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Review the verb to be: I am, you/we/they are, he/she/it is.
- New verb have 🫳 (иметь); grammar "I have ___".
- Words: apple 🍎 (яблоко), toy 🧸 (игрушка), sister 👧 (сестра).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: ten, net, pen. Three words.

**2. Match the pair.** I say a pronoun, you add the right to-be word: I → am, he → is, we → are, she → is, they → are.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I am Nika.   She is kind.   We are happy.   I have a toy.   I have an apple.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "I have a ___" and swap: toy, book, cat, ball. Four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me three things you really have: "I have a ___." (a sister, a toy, a book).

> 🇷🇺 Choosing am/is/are is new because Russian uses no verb here at all. Little
> rhyme: I → am, he/she/it → is, you/we/they → are. Before a vowel sound we say an,
> not a: an apple.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Say the to-be rhyme once a day: "I am, he is, we are."
- Say three "I have a ___" sentences about real things.
