# Lesson 9 — Do you? and I don't 🤔

> Nika learns to ASK and to say NO: "Do you like ___?" and "I don't ___."

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review the verb -s (4 min)
2. New words — do, don't (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — questions and negatives (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Ask a question with the helper word do: Do you ___?
- Say no: I don't ___ (don't = do not).
- Words: swim 🏊 (плавать), read 📖 (читать).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: do, go, no. Three words.

**2. Yes or no.** I ask "Do you like ___?" — you answer "Yes, I do." or "No, I don't." Try apples, dogs, swimming.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> Do you like dogs?   Yes, I do.   Do you swim?   No, I don't.   I don't eat fish.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "Do you like ___?" and swap: dogs, apples, books, cats. Ask four questions.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me one thing you do and one you don't: "I like ___." / "I don't like ___."

> 🇷🇺 English uses a helper word do to make questions and no-sentences — there is
> nothing like it in Russian, so it feels odd at first. Don't is just do + not
> squashed together.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Ask someone two "Do you like ___?" questions a day.
- Say one true "I don't like ___." sentence.
