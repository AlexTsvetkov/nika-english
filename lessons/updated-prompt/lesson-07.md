# Lesson 7 — Action verbs 🏃

> Now Nika DOES things with words! She learns action verbs and the pattern
> "I / you / we + verb".

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review "I have" (4 min)
2. New words — run, eat, like, play (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "I + verb" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Action verbs: run 🏃 (бегать), eat 🍽️ (есть), like 💛 (любить), play 🎲 (играть).
- Grammar "I / you / we + verb" — the verb stays the same.
- Word: jump 🦘 (прыгать).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: run, sun, cut. Three words.

**2. Act-it-out.** Read a verb and DO it: run, eat, jump, play. Say the word as you move.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I run.   I eat.   You play.   We like dogs.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "I ___" and swap the verb: run, eat, play, jump. Say four sentences and act each one.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me three true things you do and like: "I like ___." / "I play ___." / "We eat ___."

> 🇷🇺 Good news: with I / you / we / they the verb does not change — I run, you
> run, we run, all the same word. That changes for he/she next lesson! Watch the
> English /r/ in run — softer than the Russian «р», no roll of the tongue.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Say three "I ___" action sentences a day and act them out.
- Tell someone one thing you like: "I like ___."
