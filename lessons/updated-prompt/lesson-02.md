# Lesson 2 — You 🫵

> Nika turns to talk TO someone. She learns "you are ___" and two golden
> questions: "What's your name?" and "How are you?"

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review "I am" (4 min)
2. New words — you, name, fine, kind (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "You are ___" + two questions (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- The pronoun you (ты/вы) and grammar "You are ___".
- Ask and answer two questions: What's your name? / How are you?
- Words: name 🏷️ (имя), fine 🙂 (хорошо), kind 🤗 (добрый), tall 📏 (высокий).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: it, sit, pin. Three words.

**2. Point and say.** Point at me and say "You are ___": kind, tall, big. Then point at a toy and do the same.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> You are kind.   What's your name?   I am Nika.   How are you? — I am fine.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "You are ___" and swap: kind, tall, big, good. Four sentences, pointing each time.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Ask me the two questions and answer back: "What's your name?" / "How are you?"

> 🇷🇺 Same surprise as last time: English needs are — You are kind, never "You
> kind". And "What's" is short for "what is" — the little mark ' just glues the two
> words together.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Ask one person a day: "What's your name? How are you?"
- Say two "You are ___" sentences to someone kind.
