# Lesson 19 — Where is it? 📦

> Nika says where things are with in, on, under and asks "Where is ___?"

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review adjectives (4 min)
2. New words — place words (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "It is ___ the box" / "Where is ___?" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Say where things are: in, on, under.
- Ask and answer "Where is ___?"
- Words: in 📥 (в), on ⬆️ (на), under ⬇️ (под), box 📦 (коробка).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: in, on, under. Three place words.

**2. Hide and say.** Put a toy in, on, and under a box. Each time say where: "It is in the box. It is on the box. It is under the box."

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> The cat is in the box.   The ball is on the box.   The toy is under the box.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "It is ___ the box" and swap in: in, on, under. Then ask "Where is the cat?" and answer.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Look around and tell me where three real things are: "My bag is on the chair. My shoes are under the bed."

> 🇷🇺 Small place words carry big meaning: in / on / under. Russian uses cases
> and changes word endings for place; English keeps the thing the same and just
> puts the little word in front. Watch the /θ/ in "the" (soft tongue-tip sound,
> not /z/ or /d/) — it comes up a lot here.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each day say where two things are: "It is in / on / under the ___ ."
- Ask a parent "Where is ___?" and answer together.
