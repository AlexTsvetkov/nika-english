# Lesson 20 — All about me 🌟

> The Phase 2 finish! Nika puts it all together to tell everything about
> herself: family, her day, her things, and what she can do.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review in/on/under (4 min)
2. Review words — mix from Lessons 11–19 (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "All about me" sentences (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Put it all together: name, family, things, abilities, day.
- Mix the grammar from Phase 2 in one little talk.
- Words: me 🙋 (я), family 👨‍👩‍👧 (семья), favourite ⭐ (любимый), everything 🌈 (всё).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds from the whole phase: cats, blue, swim, playing. Mixed review.

**2. Quick-fire.** Answer fast: "How many fingers?" "What colour is your book?" "Can you jump?"

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I am Nika. I have got a pet. I can swim. It is blue. I am playing now.

**4. Swap game.** Build your own five sentences, one from each topic: a colour, a number, have got, can, and -ing.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me "All about me" — five true sentences: who you are, your family, your things, what you can do, what you are doing now.

> 🇷🇺 Huge milestone! Nika now has plurals, numbers, colours, have got, can/can't,
> present continuous, food, adjectives and place words — on top of the whole
> Phase 1 (pronouns, to be, present simple). Celebrate: she can introduce herself
> and describe her world in English. Keep the little talk going for fun.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each day say your "All about me" talk — five sentences.
- Add one new sentence whenever you learn a new word.
