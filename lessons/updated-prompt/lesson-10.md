# Lesson 10 — My day 🌞

> The big finish! Nika uses the present simple to tell her whole day:
> "I get up. I eat. She plays."

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review do/don't (4 min)
2. New words — get up, wash, go, dinner (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — tell my day in order (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Tell a daily routine with the present simple: I get up, I eat, I play.
- Words: get up ⏰ (вставать), wash 🚿 (мыться), go 🚶 (идти), dinner 🍽️ (ужин).
- Remember he/she adds -s: She plays.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: get, up, wash. Three words.

**2. My day in order.** Say four things you do each day, in order: "I get up. I wash. I eat. I go to school."

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I get up.   I eat breakfast.   I go to school.   She plays.   We eat dinner.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "I ___" and tell more of your day: get up, wash, eat, play. Four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me your REAL day from morning to night — five sentences, start to finish.

> 🇷🇺 This is the present simple — everyday actions. Nika now has it all: pronouns
> + am/is/are + verbs (with the he/she -s). Celebrate — she can talk about her whole
> day in English!

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each morning, say three things: "I get up. I wash. I eat."
- Each night, tell your day in five sentences.
