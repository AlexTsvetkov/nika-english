# Lesson 14 — I have got 🎒

> Nika says what she owns with "have got" and asks "Have you got ___?"

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review colours (4 min)
2. New words — things you own (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "I have got ___" / "Have you got ___?" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Say what you own: "I have got ___".
- Ask "Have you got ___?"
- Words: pet 🐹 (питомец), bike 🚲 (велосипед), ball 🏀 (мяч), bag 🎒 (сумка).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: got, bag, ball. Short words, listen to the end sound.

**2. Show and tell.** Hold up real things and say: "I have got a ball. I have got a bag."

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I have got a pet.   I have got a bike.   Have you got a ball? Yes, I have.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "I have got a ___" and swap in: pet, bike, ball, bag. Four sentences, then ask me "Have you got a ___?"

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me three things you really have: "I have got a ___ ." Then ask me what I have got.

> 🇷🇺 "Have got" means own, same as "I have". Russian uses "у меня есть" with no
> verb like this, so the two words have + got together feel odd — teach the whole
> chunk "have got" as one thing. For the question, got jumps to the front: "Have you got...?"

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each day name two things: "I have got a ___ ."
- Ask a parent: "Have you got a ___?"
