# Lesson 4 — It and This 👉

> Nika names things! She learns it and this and the pattern "It is a ___" for
> animals and objects.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review he/she (4 min)
2. New words — it, this, cat, dog, ball (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "It is a ___" / "This is a ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- The words it (оно) and this (это); grammar "It is a ___".
- Name things: cat 🐱 (кошка), dog 🐕 (собака), ball ⚽ (мяч), book 📖 (книга).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: cat, dog, pot. Three words.

**2. Grab and name.** Pick up real objects and say "This is a ___": ball, book, cup, pen.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> This is a cat.   It is a dog.   This is a ball.   It is a book.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "It is a ___" and swap: cat, dog, ball, book. Four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Walk around your room and name four real things: "This is a ___."

> 🇷🇺 English puts a little word a before a thing: a dog, a book. Russian has no
> such word («собака» stands alone) — so a is easy to forget. Say it on purpose.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Name five things in the house: "This is a ___."
- Point out of the window and say what you see: "It is a ___."
