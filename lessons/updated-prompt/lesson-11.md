# Lesson 11 — One and many 🐾

> Phase 2 begins! Nika makes plurals: one cat → three cats, and points
> at a group: "These are cats."

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review "This is a ___" (4 min)
2. New words — more things + the plural -s (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "These are ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Make plurals with -s: one toy, two toys.
- Point at many things: "These are ___".
- Words: toy 🧸 (игрушка), book 📚 (книга), apple 🍎 (яблоко), car 🚗 (машина).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: toy, toys, car, cars. Hear the -s hiss at the end.

**2. One or many?** Hold up one thing, then two. Say both: "a book / two books", "a car / three cars".

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> This is a cat.   These are cats.   This is a toy.   These are toys.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "These are ___" and swap in: books, apples, cars, toys. Four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Point at things in your room and say two true sentences: "These are my toys. These are my books."

> 🇷🇺 English just adds -s for more than one: cat → cats. And watch the pair:
> this → these for many. Russian changes whole endings, so the simple English
> -s is good news — remind her it is a soft hiss at the end.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Count things at home and say the plural: "two books, three toys."
- Say one "These are ___" sentence each day about real things.
