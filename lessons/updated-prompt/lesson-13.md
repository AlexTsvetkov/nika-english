# Lesson 13 — Colours 🌈

> Nika names colours and answers "What colour is it? It is ___."

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review numbers (4 min)
2. New words — colours (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "It is ___" / "What colour is it?" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Name colours and answer "What colour is it?"
- Grammar "It is + colour".
- Words: red 🔴 (красный), blue 🔵 (синий), green 🟢 (зелёный), yellow 🟡 (жёлтый).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: red, blue, green. Three colour words.

**2. Colour hunt.** Point at things around you and name the colour: "red", "blue", "green", "yellow".

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> What colour is it? It is red.   The car is blue.   The apple is green.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "It is ___" and swap in colours for things you see: red, blue, green, yellow. Four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me the colour of three real things: "My book is red. My toy is blue."

> 🇷🇺 One word covers it for both boy and girl things — English colours never
> change endings like Russian (красный / красная / красное). Just "it is red"
> every time. Watch yellow — the y sounds like /j/ (as in "yes"), not a vowel.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each day name the colour of three things: "It is ___ ."
- Ask a parent "What colour is it?" and answer together.
