# Lesson 16 — Right now! 🏃

> Nika tells what is happening right now with "I am ___ing".

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review can/can't (4 min)
2. New words — actions happening now (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "I am ___ing" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Say what is happening now: "I am ___ing".
- Add -ing to action words: play → playing.
- Words: playing 🧩 (играю), eating 🍽️ (ем), running 🏃 (бегу), reading 📖 (читаю).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: play, playing; eat, eating. Hear the -ing ending.

**2. Mime now.** Start an action and say it: pretend to eat and say "I am eating." Then "I am reading."

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I am playing.   I am eating.   She is running.   We are reading.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "I am ___ing" and swap in: playing, eating, running, reading. Four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Do a real action and tell me as you do it: "I am sitting. I am smiling. I am talking."

> 🇷🇺 This is the "now" tense: am/is/are + verb + -ing, two parts together.
> Russian says it with just one word, so remember English needs both: "I am
> eating", never only "I eating". It is the am/is/are from Lesson 6 plus -ing.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each day do an action and say it live: "I am ___ing."
- Watch a parent and say what they are doing: "You are ___ing."
