# Lesson 18 — Big and small 🧸

> Nika describes things with adjectives: "It is a big ___ / a small ___."

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review I want (4 min)
2. New words — describing words (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "It is a ___ ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Describe things: "It is a big dog."
- Put the describing word before the thing.
- Words: big 🐘 (большой), small 🐭 (маленький), hot 🔥 (горячий), cold ❄️ (холодный).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: big, small, hot, cold. Four describing words.

**2. Big or small?** Point at real things and say: "a big book", "a small toy", "a hot drink".

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> It is a big dog.   It is a small cat.   The milk is cold.   The soup is hot.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "It is a ___ ___" and swap in: big dog, small cat, big car, small ball. Four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Describe three real things with a describing word: "My bag is big. My toy is small."

> 🇷🇺 In English the describing word comes BEFORE the thing and never changes:
> "a big dog", "a big cat", "big dogs" — always just "big". Russian changes the
> ending to match (большой / большая / большие); English keeps it still. Also
> note "a" before the describing word: "a big dog".

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each day describe two things: "It is a ___ ___ ."
- Find one big and one small thing and name them.
