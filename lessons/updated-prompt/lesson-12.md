# Lesson 12 — Numbers 1–20 🔢

> Nika counts to twenty and asks "How many?" to count real things.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review plurals -s (4 min)
2. New words — numbers 1–20 (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "How many ___? — ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Count 1–20 out loud.
- Ask and answer "How many ___?"
- Words: one 1️⃣ (один), ten 🔟 (десять), many 🤲 (много), count 🧮 (считать).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: one, two, three, four, five. Then keep going to ten.

**2. Count the room.** Count real things out loud: fingers to ten, then books and toys up to twenty.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> How many cats? Three cats.   How many books? Five books.   How many apples? Ten apples.

**4. Swap game.** Point at groups and ask yourself "How many ___?" then answer with a number: toys, cars, apples. Three times.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Count your real things and tell me: "I have five books. I have three toys."

> 🇷🇺 Numbers stay the same word every time — no changing endings like Russian
> (один / одна / одно). Watch the tricky ones: three has the /θ/ sound Russian
> lacks (tongue between teeth), and the /r/ in three and four is soft, not rolled.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Count to twenty out loud each day.
- Pick a group of things and say: "How many? ___ ."
