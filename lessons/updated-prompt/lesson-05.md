# Lesson 5 — We and They 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

> Nika talks about GROUPS now. She learns we and they and the pattern
> "We are ___", plus how English adds -s for more than one.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review it/this (4 min)
2. New words — we, they, plurals with -s (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "We are ___" / "They are ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Pronouns we 👫 (мы) and they 👬 (они); grammar "We/They are ___".
- Plurals — add -s for more than one: cats 🐱 (кошки), dogs 🐕 (собаки).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: dog, dogs, cat, cats. Hear the -s at the end.

**2. One or many?** Hold up one toy ("a cat"), then two ("two cats"). Say both and stretch the -s.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> We are happy.   They are big.   We are friends.   They are cats.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "We are ___" and swap: happy, kind, big, friends. Four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Say two true "We are ___" sentences about you and your family: "We are a family." / "We are happy."

> 🇷🇺 For more than one, English adds -s: cat → cats. Russian changes the whole
> ending differently — just remember the English -s hiss at the end. And are is
> used for we and they: We are, They are.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Count toys and say the plural: "two cats, three dogs."
- Say one "We are ___" sentence about your family each day.
