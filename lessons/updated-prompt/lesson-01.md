# Lesson 1 — Hello! 👋

> Our very first talking lesson. Nika learns to greet people and to say
> "I am ___" about herself. Quick, cheerful, lots of speaking.

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + wave hello (4 min)
2. New words — hello, hi, bye, I, am (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "I am ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Greet people: hello 👋 (привет), hi 👋 (привет), bye 👋 (пока).
- The pronoun I (я) — the word for myself; grammar "I am ___".
- Describe me: happy 😊 (счастливый), big 🧍 (большой), small 🤏 (маленький), good 👍 (хороший).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Read these slowly, slide the sounds: at, sat, tap. Three words — cheer each one!

**2. Big hello.** Wave and say hello and hi to three toys. Then say bye as they "leave". Loud and happy.

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I am Nika.   I am big.   I am happy.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "I am ___" and swap the last word: happy, big, small, good. Say four sentences.

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me two true sentences about YOU: "I am Nika." and "I am eight."

> 🇷🇺 In Russian we say «Я Ника» with no verb. In English the little word am is
> required: I am Nika, never "I Nika". This feels strange at first — practise it a
> lot. Also I is always a big capital letter.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each morning, wave and say: "Hello! I am Nika."
- Say one new "I am ___" sentence a day (happy / big / good).
