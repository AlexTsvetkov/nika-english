# Lesson 15 — I can! 💪

> Nika says what she is able to do with "I can ___" and "I can't ___".

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — read sounds + review have got (4 min)
2. New words — action verbs for abilities (5 min)
3. Pattern + swap — "I can ___" / "I can't ___" (6 min)
4. Exercises (8 min)
5. Homework (2 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Say your abilities: "I can ___".
- Say what you cannot do: "I can't ___".
- Words: swim 🏊 (плавать), jump 🤸 (прыгать), sing 🎤 (петь), fly 🕊️ (летать).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Reading warm-up.** Slide the sounds: can, swim, jump. Then stretch can't.

**2. Act it out.** Do an action and say it: jump and say "I can jump!", then "I can sing!"

**3. Pattern box — read it out loud.**
> I can swim.   I can jump.   I can't fly.   Can you sing? Yes, I can.

**4. Swap game.** Keep "I can ___" and swap in: swim, jump, sing, run. Four sentences. Then say one "I can't ___".

**5. Say it about your real life.** Tell me three true things: two you can do and one you can't: "I can ___ . I can't ___ ."

> 🇷🇺 "Can" never changes and the next verb has no -s and no "to": "I can swim"
> (not "I can to swim", not "I can swims"). The /æ/ in can and the "ca-nt" in
> can't are both hard for Russian ears — say them slowly and clearly.

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Each day do an action and say "I can ___ !"
- Say one thing you can't do yet: "I can't ___ ."
