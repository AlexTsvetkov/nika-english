# Lesson 14 — our new pattern: "I have got ___ / Have you got ___?"

Today Nika says what she owns: **I have got a dog. Have you got a pet?** (у меня есть собака; у тебя есть питомец?).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — colours from Lesson 13 + "I have ___" from Lesson 6.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — "have got" to own things; ask "Have you got ___?". Read the table.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika says what she really has.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **I have got ___ / Have you got ___?**
- Words: have got 🎒 (есть у меня), pet 🐶 (питомец), sister 👧 (сестра), ball ⚽ (мяч), bike 🚲 (велосипед).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud:**

| I | have got | a dog |
| You | have got | a ball |
| Have you got | a bike? | — Yes, I have |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "I has got a dog." → say it the right way (I/you take "have got": I have got a dog).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- у меня есть мяч → I have got a ball … have you got a pet? → у тебя есть питомец?

**4. Matching game** — match the question to its answer:
- Have you got a dog? / Have you got a bike?  →  Yes, I have. / No, I have not.

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika says 3 true things she owns:
- I have got a ___ . / I have got a ___ . / I have got a ___ .

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): say 5 things you have got, then ask a family member "Have you got ___?".

> 🇷🇺 Russian says "у меня есть" with no verb like this; English uses the little pair "have got". For a question, flip it: "Have you got ___?"
