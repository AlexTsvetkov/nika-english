# Lesson 2 — our new pattern: "You are ___"

Today Nika learns to talk TO someone: **You are ___** (ты ___), and reviews "I am ___".

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (3 min) — our old pattern: I am Nika, I am happy, I am eight.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — read the table together, point at each word.
3. Drill + swap (11 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika talks to the parent.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **You are ___**
- Words: you (ты), are (есть), nice 🙂 (хороший), here 📍 (здесь), tall 📏 (высокий), mum 👩 (мама).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud, swapping the last word:**

| I | am | happy |
| You | are | nice |
| You | are | here |
| You | are | tall |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "You nice." → say it the right way (add the missing word *are*).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- ты → you … ты здесь → you are here … you are nice → ты хороший

**4. Matching game** — match the word to its meaning:
- you • nice • here  ↔  здесь • ты • хороший

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika points at mum and says 3 sentences:
- You are nice. / You are tall. / You are here.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): say to a family member — You are nice, You are here, You are tall. Then one about yourself: I am ___.

> 🇷🇺 Same idea, two words: **am** goes with *I*, **are** goes with *you*. Russian has no "to be" word in the present, so this feels strange — drill it. The /r/ in *are* is soft, made at the back of the mouth.
