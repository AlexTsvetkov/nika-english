# Lesson 11 — our new pattern: "one cat / three cats — These are ___"

Today Nika makes words mean MORE than one: **one cat / three cats, These are cats** (один кот / три кота, это коты). Add *-s* for many!

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — the whole am/is/are chart + "This is a ___" from Lesson 4.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — one thing takes "is", many things take "are" and *-s*. Read the table.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika counts her real things.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **one ___ → two/three ___s**, and **These are ___**.
- Words: one 1️⃣ (один), many 🔢 (много), cats 🐈 (коты), books 📚 (книги), apples 🍎 (яблоки).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud, one then many:**

| This is | a cat | These are | three cats |
| This is | a book | These are | two books |
| This is | an apple | These are | five apples |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "These are a cats." → say it the right way (many things take no "a", just the *-s*: These are cats).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- три кота → three cats … these are books → это книги … два яблока → two apples

**4. Sorting game** — one or many? Put each word in its box:
- cat, cats, book, apples, dog, dogs  →  one / many

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika points at her real things and says 3 lines:
- These are my books. / These are my toys. / These are my pens.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): point at things and say one then many — one cat, three cats; one book, two books. Hiss the *-s*.

> 🇷🇺 Russian changes the END of the word in many hard ways; English is easy — mostly just add *-s* and say the little hiss. "These are" (not "this is") when there are many.
