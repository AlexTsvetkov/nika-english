# Lesson 8 — our new pattern: "He / She + action + s"

Today the tricky block: with he or she the action word gets an **-s**: **He plays / She runs** (он играет / она бегает).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — I / you / we + action word; am/is/are chart.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — read the table together, point at the extra *s*.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika talks about her family.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **He / She + action + s**
- Words: plays ⚽ (играет), runs 🏃 (бегает), eats 🍽️ (ест), reads 📖 (читает), sleeps 😴 (спит).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each pair aloud and hear the extra s:**

| I | play |
| He | plays |
| She | runs |
| He | eats |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "She play." → say it the right way (he/she add *-s*: she plays).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- он спит → he sleeps … she reads → она читает … I run → я бегаю

**4. Sorting game** — send each sentence to the right box:
- I run, she runs, he eats, we play  →  no-s box (I/we) or add-s box (he/she)

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika makes 3 sentences about family:
- My mum reads. / My dad runs. / My cat sleeps.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): say 5 "He ___s / She ___s" sentences about your family.

> 🇷🇺 One rule to remember: **he/she/it add -s** (she plays), but **I/you/we do not** (I play). Reading tip: watch the look-alikes *p* and *n* in words like *play* and *pen*.
