# Lesson 3 — our new pattern: "He is ___ / She is ___"

Today Nika talks about one other person: **He is ___ / She is ___** (он ___ / она ___).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (3 min) — our old patterns: I am ___, You are ___.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — read the table together, point at each word.
3. Drill + swap (11 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika talks about her family.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **He is ___ / She is ___**
- Words: he (он), she (она), is (есть), boy 👦 (мальчик), girl 👧 (девочка), kind 💛 (добрый).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud, swapping the last word:**

| He | is | a boy |
| She | is | a girl |
| He | is | kind |
| She | is | happy |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "She are nice." → say it the right way (he/she take *is*, not *are*).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- он → he … она добрая → she is kind … he is tall → он высокий

**4. Sorting game** — put each person in the right box:
- mum, dad, boy, girl  →  HE box (он) or SHE box (она)

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika makes 2 sentences about family:
- My mum: She is kind. / My dad: He is tall.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): say 3 sentences about family — He is ___, She is ___.

> 🇷🇺 Now we have three forms of the same little word: **am** (I), **are** (you), **is** (he/she). Russian uses none of them in the present. Reading tip: don't mix up the look-alikes *h* and *n*.
