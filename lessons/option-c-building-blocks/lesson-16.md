# Lesson 16 — our new pattern: "I am ___ing (right now)"

Today Nika says what is happening NOW: **I am playing. She is eating** (я играю сейчас; она ест сейчас).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — am/is/are chart + "can / can't" from Lesson 15.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — am/is/are + action + *-ing* for right now. Read the table.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika says what she is doing now.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **I am ___ing** (and he/she is ___ing) — happening now.
- Words: playing 🧸 (играю), eating 🍽️ (ем), reading 📖 (читаю), running 🏃 (бегу), sleeping 😴 (сплю).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud:**

| I | am | playing |
| She | is | eating |
| They | are | running |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "I playing now." → say it the right way (keep the am/is/are: I am playing now).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- я читаю → I am reading … she is sleeping → она спит сейчас … они бегут → they are running

**4. Matching game** — match the person to the *-ing* line:
- I / She / They  →  is eating / am playing / are running

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika says 3 lines about right now:
- I am sitting. / I am learning English. / I am smiling.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): watch family and say what each is doing — "He is ___ing. She is ___ing. I am ___ing."

> 🇷🇺 This is for RIGHT NOW. Keep BOTH parts: the am/is/are AND the *-ing*. Don't drop the little "am/is/are" like Russian does.
