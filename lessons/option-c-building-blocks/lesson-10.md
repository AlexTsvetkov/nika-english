# Lesson 10 — all our blocks together: "My day" (I get up, I eat, she plays)

Today Nika puts EVERY block together to tell her day: **I get up, I eat, she plays** (я встаю, я ем, она играет).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — big flashback: am/is/are, I have / I like, I play / she plays, I don't / Do you?
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — line up action words to tell a whole day. Read the table.
3. Drill + build the day (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika tells her real day.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our pattern put together: **person + action (+ s for he/she)** across a whole day.
- Words: get up ⏰ (встаю), wash 🚿 (умываюсь), eat 🍳 (ем), go 🏫 (иду), sleep 😴 (сплю).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read the day aloud, top to bottom:**

| I | get up |
| I | wash |
| I | eat |
| I | go to school |
| She | plays |
| I | sleep |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "She play and I eats." → say it the right way (She plays and I eat — the *-s* is only for she).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- я встаю → I get up … she sleeps → она спит … I go to school → я иду в школу

**4. Sorting game** — put the day in time order (morning to night):
- sleep, get up, eat, go to school  →  order them

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika tells her REAL day in 4–5 sentences:
- I get up. / I eat. / I go to school. / I play. / I sleep.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): tell your whole day in English — at least 5 sentences. Add "She plays / He eats" about family.

> 🇷🇺 Finish line! Everything connects: the to-be family (am/is/are), have/like, plain actions, the he/she **-s**, and don't/do. Nika can now describe her whole day in English — well done.
