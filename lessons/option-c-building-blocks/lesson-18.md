# Lesson 18 — our new pattern: "It is a big ___ / a small ___"

Today Nika describes things: **It is a big dog. It is a small cat** (это большая собака; это маленький кот). The describing word goes BEFORE.

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — colours from Lesson 13 + "It is a ___" from Lesson 4.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — put the describing word before the thing. Read the table.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika describes her real things.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **It is a + describing word + thing** (a big dog, a small cat).
- Words: big 🐘 (большой), small 🐭 (маленький), hot 🔥 (горячий), cold ❄️ (холодный), new ✨ (новый).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud:**

| It is | a big | dog |
| It is | a small | cat |
| It is | a new | bike |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "It is a dog big." → say it the right way (describing word first: It is a big dog).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- большая собака → a big dog … it is a cold apple → холодное яблоко … новый → new

**4. Matching game** — match the describing word to its opposite:
- big, hot, new  →  cold / small / old

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika describes 3 real things:
- It is a big ___ . / It is a small ___ . / It is a new ___ .

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): point at 5 things and describe each — "It is a big ___ / a small ___ / a new ___."

> 🇷🇺 In English the describing word comes BEFORE the thing (big dog), and it never changes its ending — always just "big", "small". One easy order every time.
