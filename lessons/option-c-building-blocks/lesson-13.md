# Lesson 13 — our new pattern: "What colour is it? — It is ___"

Today Nika names colours and asks **What colour is it? It is red** (какого цвета? оно красное).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — numbers 1–20 and "How many ___?" from Lesson 12.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — ask the colour, answer "It is ___". Read the table.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika names colours of her real things.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **What colour is it? — It is ___**.
- Words: red 🟥 (красный), blue 🟦 (синий), green 🟩 (зелёный), yellow 🟨 (жёлтый), black ⬛ (чёрный).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud:**

| What colour is it? | It is | red |
| What colour is it? | It is | blue |
| What colour is it? | It is | green |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "It red." → say it the right way (one thing takes "is": It is red).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- синий → blue … it is yellow → оно жёлтое … зелёный → green

**4. Matching game** — match the colour word to the picture:
- red, blue, green, yellow  →  🟩 / 🟨 / 🟥 / 🟦

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika points at real things and says 3 lines:
- It is red. / My bag is blue. / My pen is black.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): point at 5 things and say "What colour is it? It is ___."

> 🇷🇺 In English the colour word does NOT change for boy/girl/thing like in Russian — always just "red", "blue". One simple word every time.
