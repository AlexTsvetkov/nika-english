# Lesson 20 — all our Phase 2 blocks together: "All about me"

Today Nika puts EVERY new block together to talk all about herself: **I have got, I can, I am ___ing, I want** (у меня есть, я умею, я делаю сейчас, я хочу).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — big flashback: plurals, numbers, colours, have got, can, -ing, want, adjectives, places.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — line up all the blocks to make a little "All about me". Read the table.
3. Drill + build (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika gives her real "All about me".

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our patterns put together: **have got + can + am ___ing + want + colours/numbers/places**.
- Words: me 🙋 (я, обо мне), favourite ⭐ (любимый), together 🧩 (вместе), all 💯 (всё), now ⏱️ (сейчас).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read "All about me" top to bottom:**

| I have got | a pet |
| I can | swim |
| I am | reading now |
| I want | an apple |
| My bag is | blue |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "I can to swim and I has got a dog." → say it the right way (I can swim and I have got a dog).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- у меня есть велосипед → I have got a bike … я умею прыгать → I can jump … я хочу воду → I want water

**4. Sorting game** — send each sentence to its block (have got / can / -ing / want):
- I can run. / I want bread. / I have got a ball. / I am eating.  →  which block?

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika gives her real "All about me" in 5 sentences:
- I have got ___ . / I can ___ . / I am ___ing now. / I want ___ . / My favourite colour is ___ .

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): say your whole "All about me" — at least 5 sentences using a different block each time.

> 🇷🇺 Finish line of Phase 2! Nika can now count, colour, say what she has, what she can do, what she is doing now, and what she wants — a real little speaker. Celebrate how far she has come.
