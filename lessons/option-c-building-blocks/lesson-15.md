# Lesson 15 — our new pattern: "I can ___ / I can't ___"

Today Nika says what she is able to do: **I can swim. I can't fly** (я умею плавать; я не умею летать).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — "have got" from Lesson 14 + "I like / I play" verbs.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — "can" for things you are able to do, "can't" for not. Read the table.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika says what she really can and can't do.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **I can ___ / I can't ___** (+ Can you ___?).
- Words: can ✅ (умею), can't ❌ (не умею), swim 🏊 (плавать), run 🏃 (бегать), jump 🤸 (прыгать).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud:**

| I | can | swim |
| I | can't | fly |
| Can you | run? | — Yes, I can |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "I can to swim." → say it the right way (after "can" the plain word: I can swim).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- я умею бегать → I can run … I can't fly → я не умею летать … can you jump? → ты умеешь прыгать?

**4. Sorting game** — put each action under "I can" or "I can't" for YOU:
- swim, fly, run, jump, read, cook  →  I can / I can't

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika says 2 true "can" and 2 true "can't":
- I can ___ . / I can ___ . / I can't ___ . / I can't ___ .

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): say 5 things you can do and 2 you can't, then ask "Can you ___?".

> 🇷🇺 "Can" never takes *-s* and never takes "to" — same for I, you, he, she. Just: can + plain action word. "Can't" is short for "cannot".
