# Lesson 19 — our new pattern: "Where is it? — It is in / on / under ___"

Today Nika says where things are: **Where is the cat? It is on the box** (где кот? он на коробке). Little place words!

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — "It is a big ___" from Lesson 18 + "This is a ___".
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — ask "Where is ___?", answer with in / on / under. Read the table.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika says where her real things are.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **Where is ___? — It is in / on / under ___**.
- Words: where ❓ (где), in 📦 (в), on ⬆️ (на), under ⬇️ (под), box 🎁 (коробка).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud:**

| Where is the cat? | It is | in the box |
| Where is the cat? | It is | on the box |
| Where is the cat? | It is | under the box |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "The cat the box in." → say it the right way (place word before the thing: The cat is in the box).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- в коробке → in the box … it is under the table → он под столом … на → on

**4. Matching game** — match the place word to the picture:
- in, on, under  →  ⬆️ / 📦 / ⬇️

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika hides a toy and tells where it is, 3 lines:
- It is on the ___ . / It is in the ___ . / It is under the ___ .

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): put a toy in / on / under things and say "Where is it? It is ___ the ___."

> 🇷🇺 English puts the place word BEFORE the thing (in the box), not after like Russian can. Three tiny words — in, on, under — unlock lots of sentences.
