# Lesson 9 — our new pattern: "I don't ___ / Do you ___?"

Today Nika says NO and ASKS: **I don't ___ / Do you ___?** (я не ___ / ты ___?).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — I play / he plays; the am/is/are chart.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — read the table together, point at the helper words.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say and ask about YOU (5 min) — Nika asks the parent.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **I don't + action** and **Do you + action?**
- Words: don't (не), do (слово-вопрос), swim 🏊 (плаваю), sing 🎤 (пою), dance 💃 (танцую), fish 🐟 (рыба).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud, swapping the action word:**

| I | don't | like fish |
| I | don't | swim |
| Do | you | sing? |
| Do | you | dance? |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "I not like fish." → say it the right way (use the helper *don't*: I don't like fish).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- я не пою → I don't sing … do you swim? → ты плаваешь?

**4. Matching game** — match each piece to its meaning:
- don't • Do you...?  ↔  ты ...? • не

**5. Say and ask about YOU** — Nika says one "no" and asks one question:
- I don't ___. / Do you ___?

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): ask 3 "Do you ___?" questions and say 3 "I don't ___" sentences.

> 🇷🇺 To say "no" to an action, English uses a helper: **don't** (I don't swim). To ask, it starts with **Do** (Do you swim?). Russian just adds "не" or a question tone — English needs these little helper words.
