# Lesson 5 — our new pattern: "We are ___ / They are ___"

Today Nika talks about groups: **We are ___ / They are ___** (мы ___ / они ___). Now the whole family of am/is/are!

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — the whole chart so far: I am, You are, He/She is, It is.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — read the table together, point at each word.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika talks about her family and friends.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **We are ___ / They are ___**
- Words: we (мы), they (они), friends 👫 (друзья), home 🏠 (дома), ready ✅ (готовы).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read the whole chart aloud:**

| I | am | happy |
| You / We / They | are | friends |
| He / She / It | is | here |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "We is friends." → say it the right way (we/they take *are*).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- мы друзья → we are friends … they are home → они дома

**4. Sorting game** — send each word to am, is, or are:
- I, he, she, it, you, we, they  →  am / is / are

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika makes 3 true sentences:
- We are a family. / We are home. / They are friends.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): say the whole chart aloud — I am, you are, he is, she is, it is, we are, they are.

> 🇷🇺 One idea, three words: **am** (I), **is** (he/she/it), **are** (you/we/they). This chart is the heart of English — drill it like a little song until it is automatic.
