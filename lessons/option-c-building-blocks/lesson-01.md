# Lesson 1 — our first pattern: "I am ___"

Today Nika learns to say who she is: **I am ___** (я ___).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (3 min) — this is lesson 1, so just say the alphabet and any English words she already knows.
2. Meet our pattern (7 min) — read the table together, point at each word.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika talks about herself.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **I am ___**
- Words: I (я), am (есть), happy 😊 (счастливая), big 🧍 (большая), eight 8️⃣ (восемь).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud, swapping the last word:**

| I | am | Nika |
| I | am | happy |
| I | am | big |
| I | am | eight |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "I happy." → say it the right way (add the missing word *am*).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English:
- я → I … счастливая → happy … я счастливая → I am happy

**4. Matching game** — match the word to its meaning:
- I • happy • eight  ↔  восемь • я • счастливая

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika makes 3 true sentences:
- I am Nika. / I am eight. / I am happy.

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): say our pattern aloud 3 times — I am happy, I am big, I am eight.

> 🇷🇺 In Russian we drop the "to be": we say "я счастливая" with no word for *am*. In English the word **am** must always be there. Practise the /h/ in *happy* — a soft breath, like warming your hands.
