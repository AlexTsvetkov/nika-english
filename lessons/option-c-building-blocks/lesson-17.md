# Lesson 17 — our new pattern: "I want ___ / Do you like ___?"

Today Nika talks about food: **I want an apple. Do you like milk?** (я хочу яблоко; ты любишь молоко?).

## 📋 Plan
1. **Flashback review** (4 min) — present continuous from Lesson 16 + "I like" from Lesson 6.
2. Meet our pattern (6 min) — "I want ___" to ask for food, "Do you like ___?" to ask. Read the table.
3. Drill + swap (10 min) — do the exercises below.
4. Say it about YOU (5 min) — Nika says her real favourite food.

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Our new pattern: **I want ___ / Do you like ___?**
- Words: want 🙏 (хочу), apple 🍎 (яблоко), milk 🥛 (молоко), bread 🍞 (хлеб), water 💧 (вода).

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika

**1. Substitution table — read each row aloud:**

| I | want | an apple |
| I | want | water |
| Do you like | milk? | — Yes, I do |

**2. Fix the sentence** — this one is broken, fix it:
- "You like bread?" → say it the right way (add the little "Do": Do you like bread?).

**3. Fast swap** — parent says Russian, Nika says English, then back:
- я хочу хлеб → I want bread … do you like milk? → ты любишь молоко? … вода → water

**4. Matching game** — match the food word to the picture:
- apple, milk, bread, water  →  🥛 / 🍎 / 💧 / 🍞

**5. Say it about YOU** — Nika says 3 true lines:
- I want ___ . / I like ___ . / I don't like ___ .

## 🏠 Homework
Every day (5 min): at meals, say "I want ___" and ask family "Do you like ___?".

> 🇷🇺 For a yes/no question with "like", put the little helper "Do" in front: "Do you like ___?" Answer short: "Yes, I do / No, I don't."
