# Lesson 4 — Review + first sentences 📖✨

> No new sounds — today is all **reading practice** with `s a t i p n`, plus the word
> **I 🙋** (says its name, always a capital). A confidence lesson — lots of wins. 🎉

## 📋 Plan (≈25 min)
1. Warm-up — sound speed round, 6 cards (3 min)
2. Word reading race (8 min)
3. Sentence exercises (10 min)
4. Write + homework (4 min)

## 🎯 What we will learn
- Read `s a t i p n` words smoothly (less sounding-out, more "just reading").
- Read a whole **sentence** and the word **I 🙋**.

## ✏️ Exercises for Nika
**1. Sound Olympics.** Point to the 6 cards; she says each sound. Time it, beat the record
twice. 🏁

**2. Reading tower.** Read and stack a block for each:
`sat 🪑 · pin 📌 · tap 🥁 · tin 🥫 · nap 😴 · sip 🥤 · pit 🕳️ · pat 🤚 · ant 🐜 · pan 🍳 · spin 🌀 · snap 🫰`
Count the tower. "You read 12 words!" 🏆

**3. Odd one out.** Read **pin 📌 · tin 🥫 · tap 🥁** — which has a different vowel? (tap)
3 sets.

**4. Read & act sentences.** Point word by word; she reads then does the action:
- **I 🙋 sit 🪑.** · **I 🙋 nap 😴.** · **I 🙋 tap 🥁.** · **I 🙋 pat 🤚 a cat 🐱.**

**5. You read, I act.** Nika holds a sentence card; the parent reads *wrong* on purpose —
she corrects you. (Kids love being the teacher.) 😄

## 🏠 Homework (5 min/day)
- Read 1 sentence aloud daily (write 4 on cards, pick one).
- Copy the sentence **I 🙋 sit 🪑.** once (capital I, full stop).
