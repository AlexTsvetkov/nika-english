# 🗺️ Full Course Plan — Nika's English Adventure

**Learner:** 8 years old, Russian-speaking, beginner.
**Goal of this course:** by the end, Nika can read and write simple English words and
short sentences confidently, and feels that English is *fun*.
**Shape:** 6 months · 2 lessons/week · ≈ 48 lessons · 25–30 min each.

The course climbs a staircase. Each step uses only what came before, so Nika is always
succeeding, never drowning. Reading begins **in week 2** — she reads her first real word
by Lesson 4. Early wins build love for the subject.

---

## The three phases

```
 Phase 1  ─ Lessons  1–16 ─  LETTERS & SOUNDS       (learn all 42 sounds, read CVC words)
 Phase 2  ─ Lessons 17–32 ─  READING TAKES OFF      (digraphs, sight words, sentences)
 Phase 3  ─ Lessons 33–48 ─  READING & WRITING      (long vowels, stories, writing)
```

---

We learn letter *sounds* in the research-backed **phonics order** (not A-B-C). This order
lets Nika build real words almost immediately. We blend sounds into **CVC words**
(consonant-vowel-consonant, like `cat`, `pin`, `dog`) — the foundation of all reading.

## 🧠 The methods behind this (best practices worldwide)

- **Synthetic phonics / Jolly Phonics (UK):** teach sounds first, in a build-a-word order,
  with a hand-action and story for each sound. The global gold standard for early reading.
- **Multisensory learning (Montessori, Orton-Gillingham):** every sound is *heard, seen,
  spoken, acted, and traced* — so it sticks through many senses. Great for all children.
- **Total Physical Response (TPR):** movement + language together ("stand up", "jump").
- **Spaced repetition:** old material returns as warm-ups, forever.
- **Comprehensible input + low anxiety (Krashen):** lots of easy, fun exposure; zero
  pressure. Mistakes are welcome and normal.
- **Teaching-to-learn:** Nika teaches Lumi 🦊 each lesson — the "protégé effect".

---

## 📈 How to know it's working

- Phase 1: she says sounds automatically and blends CVC words.
- Phase 2: she reads short sentences without sounding out every letter.
- Phase 3: she writes a sentence that someone else can read.

Keep it light. If any lesson feels hard, repeat its game another day before moving on.
The staircase waits for her — there is no bell. 🔔🚫
