# 🎵 Sounds, Actions & Songs — Quick Reference

A one-page cheat-sheet for every sound in Lessons 1–10. Keep it handy while you teach.
Each sound has a **story picture**, a **hand/body action** (movement helps memory), and the
**pure sound** to make. Letter *names* are not used yet — only sounds.

| Letter | Pure sound | Story picture | Action | 🇷🇺 Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **s** | /sss/ | hissing snake 🐍 | hand wiggles like a snake | = «с», easy |
| **a** | /æ/ | ant on your arm 🐜 | fingers crawl up arm | NOT Russian «а» — bright, open |
| **t** | /t/ | tapping / rain 🥁 | tap two fingers on palm | = «т», keep it short |
| **p** | /p/ | popping lips 💨 | puff air onto your hand | looks like «р» but says /p/! |
| **i** | /ɪ/ | squeaking mouse 🐭 | wriggle, rub eyes | short /ɪ/, not «и»/«ай» |
| **n** | /nnn/ | humming plane ✈️ | arms as wings, "fly" | looks like «п» but says /n/! |
| **c** | /k/ | clicking camera 📷 | "click" a camera | looks like «с» but says /k/! |
| **k** | /k/ | flying kite 🪁 | pull a kite string | same sound as c |
| **e** | /e/ | cracking egg 🥚 | crack an egg on the table | short /e/, not «е/йэ» |
| **h** | /h/ | breath on glass 🫧 | breathe onto your palm | soft breath, NOT harsh «х»; looks like «н» |
| **r** | /r/ | running dog 🐕 | run on the spot | do NOT roll it like Russian «р» |
| **m** | /mmm/ | yummy tummy 🍫 | rub your tummy | = «м», easy |
| **d** | /d/ | banging drum 🥁 | play an air-drum | = «д», short, no «ы» |
| **g** | /g/ | glugging bottle 🍾 | tip a bottle, glug | hard «г» |
| **o** | /ɒ/ | orange / light on 🍊 | round mouth + hands circle | short round /ɒ/, not long «оу» |
| **u** | /ʌ/ | umbrella up ☔ | push umbrella up | short /ʌ/ "uh" |
| **l** | /lll/ | licking lollipop 🍭 | lick a lollipop | tongue up, softer than «л» |

> These picture-action-sound links are the Jolly Phonics method used in schools worldwide.
> Doing the **action** while saying the sound makes it stick far better than looking alone.

## 🎶 Songs & chants (sing to any simple tune)

**Hello song** (start every lesson):
> *"Hello, hello, hello to you! Hello, hello, I'm happy too!"* 👋

**Sound chant** (warm-up — point to flashcards on the beat):
> *"/s/ /a/ /t/ /i/ /p/ /n/ — say them again!*
> */c/ /e/ /h/ /r/ /m/ /d/ — read with me!"* 🎵

**Blending chant** (teaches HOW to read):
> *"Sound it, sound it, slide them slow… c-a-t, now off we go — CAT!"* 🐱

**Goodbye song** (end every lesson):
> *"Goodbye, goodbye, we learned a lot! Goodbye, goodbye — you're very smart!"* 🌟

## ▶️ Free resources worth a look (optional, for the parent)
- **Jolly Phonics / Jolly Songs** — the official sound songs & actions (search online; many
  free clips exist). Great for hearing the pure sounds if you're unsure.
- **Alphablocks** (BBC) — a lovely cartoon where letters blend into words. Short episodes
  match exactly what Nika is learning. Watch one after a lesson as a treat. 📺
- A short daily dose of English songs/cartoons (even 5–10 min) hugely helps the ear.

> Tip: you don't need any of these to run the course — the lesson files are complete on
> their own. These just add extra listening practice if you want it.
