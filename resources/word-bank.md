Cut these into cards for building, reading races, memory, and bingo. Words are grouped by
the lesson where they first become readable, so you can always match the game to what Nika
knows. Every word here is **fully decodable** with the sounds taught so far (plus the
tricky words **I / the / is**).

## 🎲 Games you can play with any list above

1. **Word Builder** — you say a word, she lays out the letter cards in order, then reads it.
2. **Reading Relay / Tower** — read words down a list; stack a block/card for each; count the tower. 🏆
3. **Swap-a-Sound** — change one letter to make a new word: `pin → tin → tip → top → hop`.
4. **Memory Pairs** — face-down word cards; flip two, read them, keep if they rhyme.
5. **Silly or Real?** — read real + nonsense words (`nit`, `vap`); she decides. Builds decoding, zero pressure.
6. **I-Spy sounds** — "I spy something with a `/s/` sound!" (around the room).
7. **Bingo** — grid of 6 words; you call a word, she finds & reads it, covers it.
8. **Act-it** — she reads an action sentence (`I run up`) and does it. 🏃
9. **Rhyme Hop** — say a rhyming chain (`hat–rat–cat–pat`), hop on each word.
10. **Treasure Hunt** — hide word cards; she finds one, reads it, earns the "treasure" (a sticker).

> Rotate 1–2 games per lesson so it never gets stale. If a game is a hit, repeat it — a
> child happily reads the same words 20 times inside a game she loves. That repetition is
> exactly what builds fluent reading. 🌟
